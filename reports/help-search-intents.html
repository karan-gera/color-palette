what changed

The index now captures tasks, colloquial phrasing, creative-app names, file formats, acronyms, alternate spellings, accessibility language, and platform shortcut vocabulary. Exact aliases are scored independently instead of being flattened into one keyword blob.

review decision

matching pipeline

normalize

lowercase, strip punctuation, fold diacritics, preserve compact equivalence

remove filler

ignore conversational words such as “how”, “do”, “my”, and “to” when meaningful terms remain

score phrases

reward an exact full-query alias before scoring individual query terms

fuzzy-match

bounded edit distance handles insertions, deletions, substitutions, and transpositions

rank

require every meaningful term, then sort by score and title

review notes and tradeoffs

curated index

Aliases are explicit and auditable. New help pages still require deliberate vocabulary maintenance.

bounded fuzziness

No search dependency was added. Short terms remain strict to limit noisy matches; longer terms tolerate up to two edits.

AND semantics

Every meaningful query term must match one page. This keeps results precise but may return nothing for mixed unrelated intents.

widest safe pass

Coverage grew to 509 aliases with natural creation, storage, sharing, and export phrasing. A collision test exposed “organize saved palettes” routing to tags; the ambiguous tag alias was removed and the collections phrase was made explicit.
